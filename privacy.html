Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dawnify processes information when you use the Dawnify iOS app or this website.

Contact

Dawnify is responsible for the information described in this policy. For privacy questions or data requests, email support@dawnify.app.

Information stored on your device

Dawnify stores alarm schedules, mission settings, timers, habits, focus settings, and preferences on your device to provide the app’s features. Screen Time selections are handled through Apple’s Family Controls framework; Dawnify receives opaque selections rather than a person’s app-usage history.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Dawnify stores the Apple user identifier and any name or email address Apple elects to share, on your device, to keep that local sign-in state. Dawnify does not use these details for advertising or sell them.

Permissions you choose to grant

Dawnify requests permissions only for features you choose to use. These can include notifications and alarm access, Screen Time, Camera, Microphone, Speech Recognition, Motion & Fitness, Photos, Apple Music/media-library access, and read-only HealthKit data for habit tracking. You can decline optional permissions and change them later in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Dawnify subscriptions and free trials are processed by Apple through the App Store. Dawnify does not collect or store payment-card details. Apple manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds under its terms for your account region.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostic collection is disabled by default. If you actively enable Share diagnostics in Dawnify Settings, the app may send crash, performance, and limited product-use information—such as device model, operating-system version, app version, crash reports, performance signals, and selected feature-use events—to Sentry and PostHog to help us improve reliability. Diagnostics are not used for advertising and are not sold. You can turn this sharing off at any time in Settings.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate Dawnify, respond to support requests, and process App Store purchases. We do not sell personal information. Apple processes App Store purchases. Sentry and PostHog receive diagnostics only after you enable Share diagnostics. We may disclose information when required by law or during a business transfer.

Retention and deletion

Most Dawnify data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. If you used Sign in with Apple, Delete Account or Sign Out in Dawnify Settings removes the locally stored sign-in identity; it does not change Apple-managed purchases or subscriptions. Support correspondence and optional diagnostics are retained only as needed to respond to your request, improve the app, and meet legal obligations. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by Dawnify, contact support@dawnify.app.

Children

Dawnify is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Dawnify’s data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
